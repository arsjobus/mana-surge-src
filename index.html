MANA SURGE

Command the Red clan against two rival AI factions. Spawn troops, seize combat shrines, and hold the Mana Pool at the center of the realm to be the first to gather 10 mana points.

How to Play Mana Surge

Be the first to collect 10 mana points from the glowing Mana Pool at the center of the board.

Turn Structure

Start — your action points refresh (1, plus bonuses) and your spawner places new troop(s) on an adjacent empty tile.
Move — spend 1 action point to move a troop one tile into an empty adjacent hex.
Combat — spend 1 action point to attack an adjacent enemy troop.
Move — a second movement window using any remaining action points.
End — if you occupy the Mana Pool, you automatically gain 1 mana point.

Combat

All troops start at 1 ATK / 1 DEF. An attack destroys the defender if the attacker's ATK is greater than or equal to the defender's DEF — and vice versa for the defender striking back at the same time. Two default troops trade evenly and both die, so simple attacks are real threats: sitting on the Mana Pool or a shrine does not make you safe from being contested!

The Board

An outer ring of stone tiles is each clan's home territory (spawners + starting troops). The inner grass hexagon is the battlefield, holding every shrine, hazard, and the Mana Pool at its heart.

Tile Types

Mana Pool — collect a point each turn you hold it (can still be attacked).
Thorns — instantly destroys any troop that steps on it.
−1 Attack shrine.
+1 Attack shrine.
+1 Defense shrine.
Extra Action shrine — +1 action point per turn while occupied (out in the stone ring).
Extra Spawn shrine — +1 troop spawned per turn while occupied.
Sacrificial Dagger — spend 1 action point to sacrifice 3 of your troops for 1 mana.

Troops standing on the Mana Pool, the Surge shrine, or the Growth shrine are locked: they cannot move or attack while there (but can still be attacked). The Sacrificial Dagger does not lock its occupant.
